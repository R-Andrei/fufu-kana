#### [Live here](https://r-andrei.github.io/fufu-kana/)

The app uses locally bundled [Furifont Sans](public/Furifont/README.md). Under **Practice
Settings → Font readings**, choose Regular (no readings), Hiragana readings,
or Romaji readings. The choice is saved in this browser; Regular is the default.
Readings annotate supported kanji and leave kana unchanged. Enable **Kanji** in
Word Practice to see them on vocabulary prompts.
